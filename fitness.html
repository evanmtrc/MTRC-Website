Common questions

I haven't exercised in a while. Where do I start?

Every new member gets a free session with a trainer to learn the equipment and find a good starting point.

Are classes okay for beginners?

Yes. Every class is multi-level, and instructors always offer easier and harder options.

How do I sign up for a class?

Use the MyClub app, call the front desk, or stop by.

Are the trainers certified?

Yes. All of our trainers are certified, and many hold several specialty certifications.

Tours are free, and new members get a free trainer session.
