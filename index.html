A day at Mt. Tam

From first light to lights out

Scroll to follow a typical day at the club.

4:45 AM

Doors open

Early risers head for the gym and the lap lanes.

Morning

Swim and stretch

Swim laps, lift, or join a morning class.

10:30 AM

Café opens

Coffee, salads and lunch in the lounge, Tuesday through Sunday.

Afternoon

Lessons and clinics

Private lessons, junior clinics and, in summer, camp on the courts.

Evening

Leagues and socials

League matches, line dancing and Bridge Night.

10 PM

Lights out

Weekday doors close at 10. Weekends run 6:45 AM to 9 PM.

Meet our tennis director

Shibu Lal

A former touring pro and the winningest player in Bangladesh's Davis Cup history. Today he runs tennis at Mt. Tam: lessons, tournaments and the pro shop.
