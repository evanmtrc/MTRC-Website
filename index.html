Meet our tennis director, Shibu Lal

Shibu is a former touring pro and a Bangladesh Davis Cup veteran, and he has been ranked in the top five nationally in three senior age groups. Today he runs tennis at Mt. Tam: lessons, tournaments and the pro shop.
