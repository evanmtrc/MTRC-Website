Meet your coach

Kevin runs pickleball at Mt. Tam, including the Saturday drop-in. He is a strong 4.0 player who also teaches yoga and personal training, and he brings focus, strategy and a calm, encouraging style to the court.

New to pickleball?

What is pickleball?

A paddle sport that mixes tennis, badminton and ping-pong, played on a small court with a light plastic ball. Rallies are short and easy to learn.

Is it good for older players?

Yes. The smaller court means less running, which is why it is popular with players of every age.

What should I bring?

Court shoes and water. If you don't have a paddle yet, ask the front desk before you come.

Want to give it a try?

Come to the Saturday drop-in, or call the front desk to book a lesson with Kevin.
