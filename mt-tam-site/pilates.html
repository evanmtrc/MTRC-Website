Ways to practice

  • Reformer: spring resistance for length, tone and posture
  • Mat: breath-driven work for core and flexibility
  • Chair and tower: functional, total-body strength
  • Private and small group: focused attention and faster progress

Group classes like Power Pilates, Stretch & Align and barre are also on the weekly schedule, free for members.

Is Pilates right for me?

Yes. Pilates suits every age and fitness level. Many members start during rehab or recovery, and our instructors know how to modify safely for injuries, chronic pain and aging joints.

Just wear comfortable, fitted clothing. We supply everything else.

Common questions

Reformer or mat: what's the difference?

Reformer adds spring resistance and gives more feedback. Mat focuses on control using your body weight and breath.

Can I start if I'm injured?

Often, yes. Tell your instructor about your injury and they will adjust the session. Check with your doctor if you're unsure.

How do I book my first class?

Call the front desk at (415) 924-6226 and we'll match you with the right class and instructor.
