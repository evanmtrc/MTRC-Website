Your instructors

Patient, certified and experienced

Hiroe Bell

Red Cross Water Safety Instructor, Masters Swim Coach

Over 25 years coaching, from total beginners to swimmers preparing for swim teams. A former member of Japan's Junior Olympic program, she has coached more than 100 swimmers who went on to USA Swimming clubs.

Vicki Stolberg

Private Swim Instructor

Teaches kids from age 3 through teens, and adults from beginners to stroke work. A former college breaststroker, Masters swimmer and Redwood High assistant coach, with experience teaching neurodivergent swimmers.

Paige Santucci

Swim Instructor, Aqua Aerobics

Ten years teaching private lessons, with a gift for helping kids feel safe and comfortable in the water. Paige also teaches aqua aerobics.

Dr. Steven Katz

Total Immersion Swim Coach

A certified Total Immersion coach who teaches smooth, efficient, mindful freestyle for adults of any age, one-on-one and in member workshops.

Common questions

Do I need to be a member?

No. Non-members are welcome at the non-member rate. Members pay less for every lesson.

What ages do you teach?

Kids from about age 3, teens, and adults, from nervous beginners to experienced swimmers working on their strokes.

Can I use my initiation credit?

Yes. New members can put their initiation spending credit toward swim lessons.

What should we bring?

A swimsuit and towel. Goggles are helpful but not required.

Is there a lifeguard?

Not during most hours, so children must always have an adult watching them outside of lessons.

Ready to get in the water?

Email Kaitlyn or the front desk and we'll find the right instructor for you.
