Common questions

Can I tour before joining?

Yes. Tours are free and there's no pressure. Call or stop by.

What exactly does each plan include?

The front desk can walk you through the details for your household.

Can guests visit?

Yes. Guests check in at the front desk for a guest pass. Some events offer guests one free visit.

How does the prepaid offer work?

Instead of the initiation fee, you pay your first 3 months of dues when you join. After that, dues are billed monthly as usual.

Do prices change?

Prices can change, so please call (415) 924-6226 to confirm current rates and offers.

Come see it in person

Tours are casual. See the courts, pools and gym before you decide.
